## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The homepage is not marketing copy about fire-cooked burgers — it IS the burger, disassembling and reassembling under the visitor's own interaction; the category default (photo hero, menu grid, footer) is refused entirely in favor of one persistent 3D mechanism carried across the whole scroll.

**OWN-WORLD:** Live-Fire Asador Grill House palette — soot-black iron `#171512`, whitewashed charred plaster `#ece4d6`, forged charcoal `#4a4642`, ember-orange `#e8611f` — rendered through a Graphic & Technical staging: sharp flat lighting, a precise grid-aligned exploded-diagram layout, thin schematic leader/guide lines connecting ingredient layers. Technical honesty carries the fire proof, not atmosphere or haze.

**STORY:** The visitor sees the whole burger, watches it precisely come apart into its 7 real ingredient layers as they scroll and point, sees the beef patty meet real fire in extreme macro, reads the numbers (180G / 100% BEEF / OPEN FIRE / 2X CHEESE) as the burger threads through them and reassembles, and lands on "View the full menu."

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Section 01 Hero — the same 7-layer burger mesh (top bun, cheese, patty, lettuce, tomato, sauce, bottom bun) centered on a near-black/graphite ground, each layer aligned to a faint technical grid, separating slightly on pointer proximity (scroll/gyroscope-tilt equivalent on touch), the nearest layer reacting most, smooth reassembly on release.

**FORM:** One persistent 3D burger + the same 7 shared ingredient meshes reused across all 7 sections (01 Hero → 07 Footer); no per-section throwaway 3D objects. Chosen comp: Graphic & Technical (option C) at `.impeccable/mocks/comp-c.png`, direction "Live-Fire Asador Grill House" (IMPECCABLE'S PICK), seed key `dc06183f`.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

### Scope note (user-pinned, overrides default surface structure)

Menu content is not a dedicated visual section on this homepage; it lives only at CTA depth ("View the full menu") and in the Footer placeholder alongside location, hours, contact, and social. Sections: 01 Hero, 02 Ingredients, 03 Fire/Patty, 04 Build, 05 Quality/Numbers, 06 Final Assembly+CTA, 07 Footer. Pointer-driven interactions (01 proximity separation, 04 magnetic spacing) get a scroll/gyroscope-tilt equivalent on touch devices. 3D asset sourcing (stock vs. custom vs. procedural) is an open decision for the production phase.
